## Prefer the general interaction model

Use interaction.requested and interaction.resolved for confirmations and other
requests for input. Use approval events when the framework provides a specific
approval state.

<span id="focus"></span>

## Announcements do not move focus

generative&#45;a11y can announce when your app needs input. Your app still opens
the dialog, manages its controls, and restores focus when it closes.
